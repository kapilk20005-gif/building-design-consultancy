Senior Structural Engineer

📍 Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad 💼 Job 📅 Posted: 3/9/2025

About the Opportunity

Experience: 4 to 6 years
Location: Mumbai (Hybrid Mode of Work)

Roles and Responsibilities

1. Project Execution

Execute structural engineering projects while ensuring design accuracy, quality, and timely delivery.

2. Optimization of Design

Review and optimize structural designs to ensure efficiency and compliance with project requirements.

3. Detailing and Drawing Checking

Detail and check drawings of structural elements using office spreadsheets.

  • Slabs: Conventional one-way, two-way, and cantilever
  • Flat slabs
  • Beams, columns, and shear walls
  • Foundations, rafts, piles, and pile caps
  • Retaining walls
  • Lift Machine Room, Overhead Tank, Sewage Treatment Plant, Underground Tank, and underground structures

4. PT Design Checking

Check post-tensioning (PT) vendors' design calculations and drawings.

5. Compliance

Ensure structural designs and project documentation comply with applicable standards and requirements.

6. Documentation

Maintain accurate project documents, design records, and technical drawings.

7. Team Responsibilities

Coordinate with team members and project stakeholders to support successful project execution.
