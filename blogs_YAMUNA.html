YAMUNA SKYCITY - MANGALORE

🔵 Published: August 13, 2025 🟢 Category: High Rise Structure.
Yamuna Skycity is one of the tallest buildings in South India. This high rise structure is part of a 45 acre gated residential complex and falls within 150 m of the Arabian Sea in Kulai, Mangalore.

Name of project : Yamuna Skycity

Location :Kulai, Mangalore

Client:Yamuna Builders

Architect : Architechnis

Area: 10 lakh sq. ft

Height: 199.75 meters

Floors: Ground + 60

Execution Partner: Ducon Contractors Pvt. Ltd.

Overview

Welcome to Yamuna Skycity, a landmark project poised to redefine the skyline of South India. Rising to a soaring height of 199.75 meters, this iconic structure is not just one of the tallest buildings in South India, but also a symbol of contemporary living blended seamlessly with thoughtful architecture.

Strategically located in Kulai, Mangalore, within 150 meters of the Arabian Sea, Yamuna Skycity is nestled in a sprawling 45-acre gated residential complex. This premium development is being brought to life by Yamuna Builders, with architectural design by Architechnis, and supported in execution by Ducon Contractors Pvt. Ltd.

Design That Elevates Living

With a total built-up area of 10 lakh sq. ft, Yamuna Skycity comprises Ground + 60 floors, each crafted to deliver luxury, functionality, and panoramic sea views.

The project begins with a podium structure of G + 3 floors, housing a multi-level car park, swimming pool, children’s play area, and an elegant two-storey clubhouse. A 50 mm expansion joint separates this podium from the main tower, ensuring structural integrity while enhancing functionality.

The ground floor of the main tower boasts a majestic triple-height entrance lobby, setting the tone for the opulence that awaits above. Apartment configurations range from 2 BHK to 5 BHK, culminating in the top 9 floors featuring luxurious 5 BHK residences – one per floor. Crowned at the top is a terrace-level open-to-sky infinity pool and event space, offering residents a one-of-a-kind elevated leisure experience.

Engineering Excellence


Materials & Framing

The superstructure employs high-grade concrete with varying strength grades—M70, M60, M50, and M40— strategically optimized across the height of the building. The raft foundation, a critical component in high-rise construction, uses M50 grade concrete to ensure long-term durability and stability.

Foundation & Piling

The site’s geotechnical profile includes weak sand, marine clay, sandy silt, and hard rock encountered between 15.1m to 23m depths. To tackle these challenging soil conditions, the foundation comprises:

495 piles of 1000 mm diameter, each capable of withstanding 607 tonnes

A 2-meter deep raft, cast in four stages using 5300 cubic metres of M50 concrete

Night-time pouring to maintain optimal curing temperatures

This meticulous foundation strategy ensures that Yamuna Skycity stands strong, even in the dynamic coastal terrain of Mangalore.

Safety First: A Culture, Not a Checklist

Given the scale and height of the project, rigorous safety protocols were a top priority. In collaboration with Ducon Contractors Pvt. Ltd., a robust safety framework was established:

A detailed Site Safety Plan was developed and communicated

Regular safety training and hands-on workshops conducted for all workers and staff

PPE kits issued to every worker, with strict compliance monitoring

Emergency protocols and first-responder training were implemented

Periodic equipment inspections ensured safe operations at all times

These measures not only ensured a safe construction environment but also helped uphold the highest standards of quality and performance.

Construction Timeline

With a clear vision and strong execution, Yamuna Skycity is progressing steadily. The next milestones include the core vertical lift, structural completion, and phased handovers.
