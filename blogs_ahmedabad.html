SARDARDHAM CAMPUS - AHMEDABAD

🔵 Published: August 6, 2025 🟢 Category: Institutional Project
SACPL is proud to be associated as the Structural Consultant for the iconic Sardardham Girls' Hostel, Ahmedabad — a flagship project under the visionary initiative of the Vishwa Patidar Samaj (VPS). This socially impactful development is part of the larger Sardardham Bhavan Complex, designed to foster educational excellence and create a strong foundation for women's empowerment.

Project Overview

Project Name: Sardardham Campus (Kanya Chhatralaya)

Location: S.P. Ring Road, Vaishnodevi Circle, Ahmedabad, India

Client: Vishwa Patidar Samaj (VPS)

Architect: MMP Architects

Built-up Area: 6,71,366.47 sq. ft.

Building Height: 2 Basement + 13 Floors

Project Category: Institutional Development

Residential Capacity: 2,000–2,500 Girl Students

Estimated Project Cost: Approximately ₹200 Crore

About Sardardham Campus

The Sardardham Campus in Ahmedabad is an important educational and residential development dedicated to supporting young women in their pursuit of academic excellence. Developed under the initiative of Vishwa Patidar Samaj (VPS), the campus represents a commitment to education, social development, and women's empowerment.

Located on S.P. Ring Road, near Vaishnodevi Circle, Ahmedabad, the project forms part of the larger Sardardham Bhavan Complex. Its extensive built-up area and multi-block development provide residential and educational facilities for thousands of students.

With architectural planning by MMP Architects, the development combines functional planning, modern infrastructure, and structural engineering to create a supportive environment for students.

Modern Facilities & Student Living

Designed to accommodate approximately 2,000–2,500 girl students, the Sardardham Girls' Hostel aims to provide a safe, comfortable, and inspiring residential environment.

The campus incorporates modern amenities and facilities designed to support students' academic and personal development, including:

Study Lounges: Dedicated spaces that encourage focused learning, preparation, and collaborative academic activities.

Dining Facilities: Dining halls designed to support the daily needs of a large residential student community.

Healthcare Support: Facilities intended to support students' health and well-being.

Indoor Recreational Zones: Spaces for relaxation, recreation, and social interaction.

High-Tech Classrooms: Learning environments designed to support modern educational activities.

Together, these facilities contribute to creating an inclusive residential campus where students can focus on education, develop new skills, and prepare for future professional opportunities.

Engineering Excellence

Large institutional developments require careful structural planning to ensure stability, durability, functionality, and long-term performance. The Sardardham Campus incorporates structural engineering considerations to support its multi-block configuration and residential requirements.

SACPL contributed structural consultancy services with a focus on structural safety, efficient load transfer, coordination with architectural requirements, and construction efficiency.

Scope of Work – SACPL Structural Contribution

As the structural engineering consultant, SACPL was responsible for providing structural design and engineering support in accordance with the project's architectural and functional requirements.

Structural Analysis and Design: Structural analysis and design for the hostel blocks to address the requirements of the multi-storey institutional development.

Earthquake-Resistant Design: Consideration of applicable seismic design requirements, including IS 1893 and the project's stated seismic zone III classification.

Load Distribution Systems: Structural planning involving flat slab and beam-slab systems, as appropriate to the building layout and design requirements.

Multidisciplinary Coordination: Coordination with architectural and MEP consultants to align structural requirements with building services and architectural planning.

Value Engineering: Evaluation of structural solutions to support durability, cost-effectiveness, and efficient construction.

Structural Safety & Design Considerations

Structural safety is a critical consideration for multi-storey residential and institutional buildings. The design process must account for the transfer of building loads, overall stability, and the interaction between structural and architectural components.

Important engineering considerations for a development of this nature include:

Load Transfer: Providing a suitable structural system to transfer dead loads, imposed loads, and other applicable design forces safely through the building.

Seismic Considerations: Evaluating the building's structural response under applicable earthquake loading requirements.

Structural Coordination: Integrating the structural framework with architectural layouts and building services.

Durability: Considering long-term structural performance and appropriate construction practices.

These engineering considerations contribute to a structural design that supports the building's intended use and operational needs.

Statue Foundation & Structural Advisory

SACPL was also honored to provide structural advisory support for the foundation and plinth design of the monumental statue associated with the development.

The statue's dimensions, material configuration, and loading characteristics make structural stability an important design consideration. Appropriate engineering assessment is necessary to support the statue and its foundation over the long term.

Statue Specifications

Statue Height: 15 metres

Pedestal Height: 2 metres

Material: Bronze covering with an internal stainless-steel frame; hollow interior.

Statue Load: Approximately 17 tonnes

Key Engineering Considerations

Wind Forces: Assessment of applicable wind loads acting on the tall, freestanding statue.

Seismic Forces: Consideration of applicable earthquake forces and their effects on the statue and supporting structure.

Foundation Stability: Structural assessment of the foundation and plinth to support the statue's weight and relevant design forces.

Architectural Integration: Coordination of the structural support system with the surrounding landscape and aesthetic requirements.

These considerations help establish a suitable engineering approach for the statue's foundation, stability, and long-term structural performance.
